Nikkah décor · Cardiff

A meaningful Nikkah setting, styled with warmth and elegance.

Imani Events creates Nikkah décor in Cardiff for couples who want the ceremony to feel personal, refined and rooted in the significance of the moment.

Ceremony styling

Designed around the way your Nikkah will actually happen.

We consider where the couple will sit, how family will gather, where photographs will happen, the flow of the room and how the décor should support the ceremony rather than overpower it.

Your design can be soft and minimal, floral and romantic, contemporary, traditional or a blend shaped around your family and venue.

Nikkah backdrop

A calm focal point for the ceremony, signatures and photographs.

Florals & aisle styling

Thoughtful floral details, candles and walkway styling to create a cohesive entrance.

Couple seating

Elegant seating arrangements coordinated with the overall stage and ceremony design.

Walima continuation

We can carry the visual story into your reception or style each event with its own identity.
