07 / A LITTLE CLARITY

A few things
you might be
wondering.

Can you design around our traditions?+

Yes. We discuss your ceremonies, family traditions and personal taste, then shape the décor around what matters to you.

What should we bring to our first conversation?+

Your event date, venue, guest numbers and a comfortable budget range are helpful. Bring any colours or photographs you love, even if your ideas are still taking shape.

Do you take care of setup?+

Yes. We prepare and style your agreed décor at the venue. Access, timings and practical details are discussed with you before the day.

Where do you work?+

We are based in Cardiff and style celebrations across South Wales and the South West, including Newport, Swansea and Bristol. Tell us about your venue if it is further afield.
