07 / BEFORE THE NEXT CHAPTER

A little clarity.
A lot of confidence.

Good questions make a beautiful beginning.
We’re here to talk through yours.

Talk about your day ↗
Can you design around our traditions?+

Yes. We discuss your ceremonies, family traditions and personal taste, then shape the décor around what matters to you.

Can we start with a budget in mind?+

Of course. Tell us the budget range you are comfortable with, your venue and the details that matter most. We can then discuss suitable décor and hire options.

What should we bring to our first conversation?+

Your event date, venue and approximate guest numbers are helpful. A favourite colour or saved photo can be a lovely starting point.

Do you take care of setup?+

Yes. We prepare and style your agreed décor at the venue. Access, timings and practical details are discussed with you before the day.

Where do you style celebrations?+

We are based in Cardiff and work across South Wales and the South West, including Newport, Swansea and Bristol. Tell us about your venue if it is further afield.

Can we personalise the décor?+

We discuss your colours, traditions and the details you want to include, then agree how to bring them into your setting.
