Planning with confidence

Questions are part
of the journey.

You do not need to have everything decided before speaking to us. These are some of the questions couples often ask when they begin.

Ask us about your day ↗
01How early should we book our wedding décor?

As soon as your date and venue are confirmed, it is worth getting in touch—especially for popular summer and weekend dates. If your celebration is closer, ask us anyway and we will let you know what is possible.

02Can you create a completely custom theme?

Yes. We can shape the colours, stage, florals, tables and finishing details around your traditions, venue and personal style rather than making your day feel like somebody else’s.

03Can you work with our venue?

We regularly style venues across Cardiff, South Wales and the South West. We can discuss the space, access and practical requirements with you before the final design is agreed.

04What if we do not know exactly what we want?

That is completely normal. Bring us your date, venue, guest numbers and any colours or images you are drawn to. We will help turn those early ideas into one clear direction.

05Do you handle setup and styling on the day?

Yes. Your agreed décor is prepared and styled at the venue so you and your family can focus on getting ready and being present for the celebration.

06Can the design be shaped around our budget?

Yes. Sharing a comfortable budget range helps us recommend the elements that will make the strongest impact and create a design that feels considered from the beginning.
