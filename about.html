About Imani Events

Celebrations with meaning & style.

Cardiff-based wedding décor and event styling created for families who want their celebration to feel both personal and beautifully put together.

Our approach

We know what

Behind every wedding are months of planning, family expectations, meaningful traditions and two people imagining one unforgettable day. We never treat that responsibility lightly. We listen first, then design a celebration that feels true to you.

With over a decade of experience in wedding and event décor, we understand the rhythm of Pakistani, Indian, Bangladeshi, Arab and wider multicultural celebrations. We respect the traditions at their heart while giving every couple a look that is entirely their own.
