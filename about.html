Our approach

Luxury without

Imani Events serves couples and families across Cardiff, Newport, Bristol, Swansea and surrounding areas. Our focus is thoughtful styling, dependable service and décor that complements the people and traditions at the heart of each event.

We particularly understand the visual language and flow of Pakistani, Indian, Bangladeshi, Arab and wider multicultural celebrations, while keeping every design individual to the client.
